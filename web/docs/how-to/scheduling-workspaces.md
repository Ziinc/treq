# Scheduling Workspaces

_Hide a workspace from the sidebar until a time you choose._

Workspace scheduling is a [Feature Preview](/docs/how-to/customizing-settings#feature-preview) control. Turn **Workspace scheduling** on in Settings → Feature Preview before the **Schedule** button appears.

## Schedule one workspace

1. Open the workspace.
2. Click **Schedule** in the workspace header.
3. Pick a preset such as 1 hour, or a date on the calendar.
4. Click **Schedule**.

Treq hides that workspace in the sidebar until the scheduled time. A calendar control on the Workspaces heading shows how many workspaces are hidden. Click it to show them again.

## Schedule a stack

On a stacked workspace, the stack card has **Schedule**. That action applies the same hidden-until time to every workspace in the stack.

## Remove a schedule

Open the workspace (turn on the hidden-workspaces toggle if it is still hidden). Click **Schedule**, then **Remove schedule**. You can also choose **Remove schedule** from the workspace context menu.
